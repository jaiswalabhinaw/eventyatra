About EventYatra

EventYatra started in Noida as an event management company. Along the way, we realized the bigger problem wasn't planning one event well — it was that people struggle to find trustworthy providers, and small event businesses struggle to be found.

So we're becoming a platform: a free place where wedding planners, decorators, caterers, photographers and every kind of event provider can list their business, and where anyone planning a wedding, birthday, bhandara, corporate event or society function can find and contact them directly — no middlemen, no commission (for now).

Our mission

Make it effortless to find the right person for any event, and help local event businesses grow — starting in Noida, and growing from there.

We're just getting started

EventYatra is brand new. If you're an event provider, join now while it's free and get early visibility. If you're planning an event, browse providers or tell us what you need.
